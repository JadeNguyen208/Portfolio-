`buddhistische-praxis-gemeinschaft.de` (no `www`) must load the site, and the Impressum
page and footer must be live and publicly reachable. The reviewer will open the bare
domain and click the link in your reply. Replying to an unchanged site gets you the same
rejection.

Reply directly to the rejection email so the case history stays attached. If the
thread is closed, use the Google for Nonprofits contact form and reference the
organisation name and domain.

## Attachments — what actually convinces a reviewer

| Document | Why it helps | Where you get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Vereinsregisterauszug** (current) | Proves the exact legal name and that the e.V. exists | Amtsgericht / [handelsregister.de](https://www.handelsregister.de) |
| **Freistellungsbescheid** | Proves charitable status — the eligibility requirement itself | Your Finanzamt (last issued notice) |
| **Domain invoice or registrar account page** | Directly links the domain to the association as registrant | IONOS / Strato / Wix / whoever hosts the site |
| Bank statement header or association letterhead | Secondary corroboration of name + address | Own records |

Send **PDF** or **JPG**. Black out bank account numbers on anything financial — Google
does not need them.
